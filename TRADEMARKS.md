# Name and conformance policy

"Bounded Authority Protocol" is a trademark of BaseLabs Holdings LLC, the steward. This policy
says how the name may be used. It does not limit the Apache License 2.0 grant for the code and
specifications.

## Using the name

- You may say that your software implements, supports or works with Bounded Authority Protocol,
  for example "implements the Bounded Authority Protocol" or "for Bounded Authority Protocol".
- Your own product, package or service name may not begin with "Bounded Authority Protocol".
- You may not use the name in a way that suggests the steward endorses, certifies or
  maintains your software, except as the conformance section allows.
- Ports and packages maintained by the steward are named by the steward. Forks are
  welcome under the Apache License; a fork takes its own name.
- The acronym BAP is not claimed.

## Conformance

An implementation may describe itself as "Bounded Authority Protocol Conformant v&lt;N&gt;" only
while:

1. it passes every case of the published conformance corpus for version &lt;N&gt;;
2. it publishes the result file of that run; and
3. it states the corpus revision it passed.

Conformance is self-certified and free. The steward gives no warranty or endorsement of
any implementation. The steward may list implementations that publish passing results,
and may remove a listing or ask that a claim be withdrawn on evidence that an
implementation no longer passes.

## Questions

Open an issue in this repository before any use this policy does not clearly allow.
